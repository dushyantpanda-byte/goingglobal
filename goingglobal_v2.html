The Playbook

10 Steps to
Export from India.

From company registration to first payment received — the right order, with every warning you need.

1
Register as an exporter IEC code, GST, RCMC — the three mandatory registrations
›
  • IEC (Importer-Exporter Code): Apply at dgft.gov.in. ₹0 fee. Takes 2–3 working days. Lifetime validity. Mandatory — no shipment clears customs without it.
  • GST Registration: Required to file LUT and claim zero-rated exports. If already registered, no new registration needed.
  • RCMC (Registration-cum-Membership Certificate): Issued by your export promotion council (APEDA for agri, EEPC for engineering). Required for RODTEP and scheme benefits.
💡 Apply for all three simultaneously — IEC is instant; RCMC takes 1–2 weeks.
⚠️ You cannot legally export without a valid IEC. Customs will reject your shipping bill.
2
Choose your export product and HS code The right HS code determines your RODTEP rate, duty drawback, and export restrictions
›
  • Search by product keyword at the DGFT Trade Portal (tradeservices.dgft.gov.in).
  • HS code determines: export duty (if any), RODTEP rate, duty drawback rate, and any licence requirement.
  • Products are classified as: Prohibited (can't export), Restricted (need licence), or Free (no restriction).
⚠️ Wrong HS code on the shipping bill is a customs offence. Get it verified by a licensed customs broker if unsure.
3
Choose your Incoterms and price your export FOB vs CIF vs DAP — who bears what cost and risk
›
  • FOB (Free On Board): Your responsibility ends when goods are loaded. Buyer pays freight and insurance. Most common for Indian exporters.
  • CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight): You pay freight and insurance to destination port. Higher invoice value → higher RODTEP base.
  • DAP (Delivered At Place): You deliver to buyer's door. Maximum control, maximum risk.
💡 For digital service exports, Razorpay supports USD/EUR collection with automatic FIRA — zero paperwork for your bank's EDPMS matching.
4
Open an export current account and EEFC account Separate your export proceeds — required for FEMA compliance
›
  • Current account with AD bank: All export proceeds must be credited through an Authorised Dealer bank. Mandatory.
  • EEFC Account (Exchange Earners' Foreign Currency): Retain up to 100% of forex earnings in USD/EUR/GBP. Use to pay import invoices, overseas subscriptions, or agent commissions without conversion.
⚠️ Accepting payments in INR from non-resident accounts, or via crypto, violates FEMA and attracts penalties of up to 3× the forex amount.
5
File a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) for GST-free exports Export without paying IGST — biggest cash flow benefit for SME exporters
›
  • File at gst.gov.in → Services → User Services → Furnish LUT. Instant processing.
  • Must be filed every financial year before your first export. One filing, valid for the full FY.
  • With LUT: export without paying IGST, claim ITC refund on inputs.
  • Without LUT: pay IGST upfront, wait months for refund — ties up working capital.
💡 File LUT on April 1st every year. Takes 5 minutes. Missing it on even one shipment means paying 18% IGST upfront.
⚠️ GST defaulters cannot file LUT — they must submit a bond with surety instead.
6
Get export finance: pre-shipment and post-shipment credit Banks offer concessional rates — often 2–4% cheaper than regular working capital
›
  • Pre-shipment credit (Packing Credit): Working capital from PO receipt to shipment. Available in INR or Foreign Currency (PCFC — cheaper, linked to SOFR).
  • Post-shipment credit: Finance against export documents. Includes LC negotiation, DP/DA bill discounting.
  • Razorpay Working Capital: Up to ₹5 Cr in 24 hours against invoice history — no collateral, integrates with ECGC NIRVIK insurance.
💡 ECGC NIRVIK cover lets banks sanction higher limits at lower collateral — because the bank's risk is 90% covered by ECGC.
7
Prepare your export documentation Commercial invoice, packing list, Certificate of Origin, shipping bill
›
  • Commercial Invoice: Must include IEC, GST number, LUT reference, HS code, Incoterms. Razorpay's invoicing tool auto-populates all fields.
  • Packing List: Gross weight, net weight, number of packages, dimensions.
  • Certificate of Origin (CoO): Required by buyer's customs. DGFT issues Form A for GSP countries; Chamber of Commerce for general CoO.
  • Shipping Bill: Filed by your CHA on ICEGATE. Primary customs export declaration.
⚠️ Description, value, and quantity must match exactly across invoice, packing list, and shipping bill. Any discrepancy causes customs examination and demurrage.
8
Get paid: choose the right payment instrument LC vs. advance payment vs. DP/DA — matching risk to reward
›
  • Advance Payment (T/T): Best for you. Common for small orders and trusted buyers.
  • Letter of Credit (LC): Bank guarantee. Most secure for large orders. Get your bank to confirm it.
  • DP (Documents against Payment): Buyer pays on sight of documents. Moderate risk.
  • DA (Documents against Acceptance): Buyer pays in 30/60/90 days. Use ECGC cover.
  • Razorpay Payment Pages: For digital service exports and e-commerce — accepts global cards, generates FIRA automatically.
9
Realise and repatriate export proceeds RBI's 9-month rule — the most critical FEMA compliance requirement
›
  • All export proceeds must be realised and repatriated within 9 months from the date on the shipping bill.
  • Your AD bank creates an EDPMS entry for every shipping bill. Inward payment must be matched to it.
  • After 9 months without payment, apply to your bank for extension with justification.
  • Razorpay auto-matches inward remittances to your invoices and generates FIRA — reducing EDPMS reconciliation from weeks to minutes.
⚠️ FEMA Section 13 penalty for non-realisation = minimum of the full unpaid amount, with compounding interest. Banks are required to report to RBI.
10
Claim your incentives: RODTEP, Duty Drawback, EPCG Most SMEs leave 2–5% of export value unclaimed every year
›
  • RODTEP: Remission of embedded state taxes (0.3%–4.3% of FOB). Declared on shipping bill by your CHA. Credited as e-scrip on ICEGATE.
  • Duty Drawback (AIR): Automatic refund of customs duty on imported inputs based on HS code. Declared at shipping bill stage.
  • EPCG: Import capital goods at 0% duty in exchange for exporting 6× the duty saved over 6 years.
⚠️ RODTEP and Duty Drawback must be declared on the shipping bill. They cannot be claimed retroactively on the same bill. Tell your CHA explicitly.

Key export
regulations.

Plain-English summaries of the six regulatory frameworks every Indian exporter must understand.

RBI

RBI Master Direction — Export of Goods & Services

The primary RBI regulation governing all exports. Covers the 9-month repatriation rule, EDPMS, write-off limits, agency commission deductions, and advance remittances.

Official Source →
FEMA

FEMA Notification 23 — Export of Goods & Services

Defines permissible export currencies, banking channels, and prohibitions. Bans export proceeds via informal channels, hawala, or cryptocurrency. All proceeds must route through AD-category banks.

Official Source →
DGFT

Foreign Trade Policy 2023–28

India's 5-year export policy. Covers IEC, RCMC, export prohibitions/restrictions, Advance Authorisation, EPCG, and DFIA. The master document for all trade incentive schemes.

Official Source →
DGFT

DGFT Handbook of Procedures 2023

Step-by-step procedures for RCMC, Advance Authorisation, EPCG, EFC filing, and more. The operational companion to FTP 2023. Updated quarterly.

Official Source →
GST

GST Zero-Rating & LUT Rules

Exports are zero-rated under IGST. Export under LUT without paying IGST and claim ITC refund — almost always better than paying IGST and claiming cash refund later.

Official Source →
GST

GST ITC Refund on Exports

Claim refund of accumulated ITC on inputs used in zero-rated supplies. File RFD-01 on the GST portal within 2 years from the relevant shipping bill date.

Official Source →
EXIM

EXIM Bank Export Finance

India's apex export finance institution. Pre-shipment credit, buyer's credit lines for foreign buyers, overseas investment loans, and lines of credit to foreign governments.

Official Source →
ECGC

ECGC Credit Risk Insurance

NIRVIK scheme covers 90% of principal and interest for MSMEs — enabling higher bank credit limits at lower collateral. Insures against buyer default, country risk, and political risk.

Official Source →
CUSTOMS

Customs Duty Drawback Rules

Refund of customs duty paid on imported inputs. All Industry Rate (AIR) is automatic. Brand Rate for high-volume exporters with specific duty data. Claimed via ICEGATE.

Official Source →
RBI

EDPMS — Export Data Processing & Monitoring System

RBI's tracking system for every export shipment vs. payment receipt. Unmatched entries older than 9 months trigger FEMA compliance action. Razorpay auto-reconciles EDPMS.

Official Source →
DGFT

Advance Authorisation Scheme

Duty-free import of inputs used in export production. Covers basic customs duty, CVD, SAD, and anti-dumping duty on eligible inputs. Subject to actual user condition.

Official Source →
DGFT

RODTEP — Remission of Duties & Taxes

Replaced MEIS in 2021. Remits embedded state taxes not covered by GST or drawback. Rates: 0.3%–4.3% of FOB value by HS code. Credits are tradeable e-scrips on ICEGATE.

Official Source →

12 tips that save
exporters money.

Practical moves most first-time exporters miss — with estimated savings on each.

Always declare RODTEP on your shipping bill

Must be declared by your CHA at filing time — cannot be claimed retroactively. Rates: 0.3%–4.3% of FOB value by HS code.

Saves 0.3%–4.3% of FOB value

File LUT on April 1st every year

LUT must be renewed annually. Filing on Day 1 of the FY ensures you never accidentally export without it and pay 18% IGST.

Saves 18% IGST on invoice value

Use PCFC instead of rupee packing credit

Pre-shipment Credit in Foreign Currency (PCFC) is 3–5% cheaper than rupee packing credit — linked to SOFR + spread.

Saves ~2–4% on working capital cost

Get ECGC cover before extending open-account terms

ECGC NIRVIK insures up to 90% of invoice value against buyer default. Premium is tax-deductible. Banks also increase your credit limits.

Protects 90% of invoice from buyer default

Confirm your LC — don't just accept it

An unconfirmed LC protects against buyer risk but not country risk. Ask your bank to add confirmation — small fee (0.25–0.75%) for complete protection.

Eliminates country / bank default risk

Apply for Advance Authorisation for inputs

Duty-free import of raw materials used in your export product. Covers basic customs duty, CVD, and often anti-dumping duty.

Saves 10–25% on imported input cost

Use EEFC account to hedge naturally

Retain up to 100% of forex in EEFC. Pay import invoices or overseas subscriptions directly from EEFC — zero conversion cost, natural hedge.

Saves 0.5–1.5% on FX conversion

Track EDPMS entries monthly, not yearly

Every unmatched entry older than 9 months triggers RBI scrutiny. Razorpay auto-reconciles remittances to shipping bills in real time.

Avoids FEMA penalty (equal to unpaid amount)

Get Brand Rate for Duty Drawback if volume is high

All Industry Rate (AIR) is lower than actual duty paid for most products. If annual exports exceed ₹5 Cr, apply for Brand Rate — refund of actual customs duty paid.

Saves 1–3% additional vs. AIR

Use MAI grant for your first trade fair

Market Access Initiative reimburses 50%–75% of stall costs at approved international trade fairs. Apply via your export promotion council 3 months in advance.

Saves 50–75% of trade fair cost

Apply for EPCG before buying capital goods

Import capital goods at 0% customs duty. Export 6× the duty saved over 6 years. For ₹1 Cr machinery, that's ₹15–25 lakh in customs savings upfront.

Saves 15–25% of capital goods cost

Register on government B2B portals for free leads

DGFT Trade Portal, Indian Trade Portal (indiantradeportal.in), and APEDA AGRI Exchange provide free export leads and buyer databases most exporters don't know about.

Generates free qualified export leads
Government Incentives

6 schemes every
exporter should use.

Highest impact, easiest eligibility for SMEs — most go unclaimed.

RODTEP

0.3%–4.3% of FOB value

Refunds embedded taxes — state levies, electricity duty, mandi tax, coal cess — not captured by GST or customs drawback. Replaced MEIS in 2021.

  1. Check your HS code rate in DGFT's RODTEP schedule
  2. Instruct your CHA to declare RODTEP on every shipping bill
  3. E-scrip credit appears in ICEGATE login within 5–7 days of LEO
  4. Transfer e-scrip or sell to a broker at near-face-value

NIRVIK — ECGC Enhanced Cover

90% credit insurance cover

Covers 90% of principal and interest for MSMEs with export credit up to ₹50 Cr. Banks sanction higher credit limits when risk is covered.

  1. Apply through your AD bank (not directly to ECGC)
  2. Bank enrolls you; premium ~0.65% p.a. of credit limit
  3. File buyer default claim within 30 days of non-payment
  4. ECGC settles 90% of principal + interest within 45 days

EPCG — Export Promotion Capital Goods

0% customs duty on capital goods

Import machinery, testing equipment, and computers at zero customs duty. Export obligation: 6× the duty saved, fulfilled within 6 years.

  1. Apply for EPCG licence at DGFT regional office before importing
  2. Import and install the capital goods
  3. Export products made using these goods (6× duty saved in 6 years)
  4. Submit EODC (discharge certificate) to DGFT on completion

MAI — Market Access Initiative

50–75% grant on market dev costs

DGFT scheme reimburses exporters for international trade fairs, buyer-seller meets, product registration abroad, and market studies.

  1. Apply through your export promotion council
  2. Activities must be pre-approved — apply 3 months before the event
  3. Submit utilisation certificate and bills after the activity
  4. Reimbursement processed within 60–90 days

IES — Interest Equalisation Scheme

2–5% interest rate subvention

RBI-administered scheme providing interest rate subvention on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit. MSMEs get 3%; merchant exporters get 2%.

  1. No separate application — automatically applied by your bank
  2. Ensure your account is tagged as "export account" under the scheme
  3. Benefit is reflected as reduced interest rate on packing credit
  4. Bank claims RBI reimbursement on your behalf

ODOP — One District One Product

Cluster-based export support

DGFT initiative to develop each of India's 739 districts as a hub for one export-potential product. Branding, certification, and buyer connection support.

  1. Check coverage at onedistrict.oneproduct.gov.in
  2. Register as ODOP producer through your district collector
  3. Access GI tagging support, quality certification assistance
  4. Apply for ODOP marketing fund via DGFT regional office

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know about going global. Still need help? Talk to Razorpay's export specialists.

Talk to Razorpay
What is the 9-month rule for export proceeds?+
Under RBI's Master Direction on Export of Goods & Services, all export proceeds must be realised and repatriated to India within 9 months from the date on the shipping bill or airway bill. For software/service exports, the 9 months runs from the invoice date. Banks track this via the EDPMS system. Failure to realise within the permitted period attracts FEMA penalties equal to the outstanding amount.
Can I receive export payment in Indian Rupees?+
Yes, but only under specific conditions — typically via Vostro accounts of foreign banks, or under special rupee trade arrangements (currently Russia, in limited cases). Payments via PayPal, Wise, or informal channels in rupees are NOT FEMA-compliant. Always use USD/EUR/GBP or other freely convertible currencies unless your CA confirms a specific rupee arrangement.
Do I need to pay GST on exports?+
No. Exports are zero-rated under the IGST Act. File a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) before your first export of each financial year — this lets you export without paying any IGST and claim a refund of input tax credit. LUT filing is instant on the GST portal. Always better than paying IGST and claiming a cash refund later.
What is the difference between RODTEP and Duty Drawback?+
Duty Drawback refunds customs duties paid on imported inputs. RODTEP refunds embedded state-level taxes (mandi tax, electricity duty, coal cess) that aren't captured by Drawback. Both can be claimed simultaneously on the same shipping bill, and both must be declared by your CHA at the time of filing — they cannot be claimed retroactively.
How does Razorpay handle FEMA compliance?+
Razorpay is an RBI-authorised Payment Aggregator. Every cross-border payment settled through Razorpay comes with a Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (FIRA) document — the proof of forex receipt your AD bank needs for EDPMS matching, SOFTEX filing, and RBI compliance. Razorpay auto-generates FIRA and matches it to your export invoices, eliminating the manual reconciliation burden.
What is SOFTEX and when do I need to file it?+
SOFTEX is the mandatory export declaration for software and IT service exports (equivalent of the Shipping Bill for goods). Filed through the STPI or NASSCOM e-SOFTEX portal — not through customs. Required for every export invoice above ₹25,000. Razorpay FIRA documents can be matched directly against filed SOFTEX entries to satisfy FEMA requirements.
Can a sole proprietor export?+
Yes. You need an IEC linked to your PAN. If annual export turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh, GST registration is mandatory. Below that threshold, registration is optional but advisable for filing LUT and claiming ITC refunds. FEMA compliance obligations are identical to any other exporter.
